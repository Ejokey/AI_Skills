NDJSON event streaming (ADR-0094)
¶
В Pooled backends worker эмитит в parent NDJSON frames:
{"type": "tool.progress", "tool_call_id": "tc-1", "message": "Step 1..."}
{"type": "tool.partial_output", "tool_call_id": "tc-1", "delta": "..."}
{"type": "tool.finished", "tool_call_id": "tc-1", "result": "..."}
Parent
WorkerPool
имеет
on_event
callback, который форвардит события в parent
SessionStore
. SSE subscribers видят их в real time через
BroadcastSessionStore
.
