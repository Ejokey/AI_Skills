Telegram super-agent
¶
aiogram 3.x driver. ADR-0061 (Phase A base) + ADR-0062 (managed bots) + ADR-0063 (bot-to-bot guards) + ADR-0064 (TG super-agent capability uplift) + ADR-0099 (sendMessageDraft live activity) + ADR-0100 (multi-chat concurrency).

подтверждения, сотрудники) — в разделе

.
